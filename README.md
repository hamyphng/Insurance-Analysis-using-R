# Insurance-Analysis-using-R
In this project, we analyze a dataset of individual health insurance records to understand what drives insurance charges. Health insurance costs vary widely across individuals, and understanding the factors that drive these differences is important for both insurers and policyholders. In this study, we analyze a dataset of 1,338 insurance records containing demographic and lifestyle information such as age, sex, BMI, number of children, smoking status, and region of residence. The motivation behind this project is to identify which characteristics most strongly influence insurance charges, helping to explain why some individuals pay significantly more than others. Our goal is to:
1. Describe and explore the variables in the dataset.
2. Identify which factors are significantly associated with insurance charges.
3. Build and interpret simple and multiple linear regression models to explain and predict medical charges.
The dependent variable is charges — the annual medical cost billed by the health insurance provider, expressed in US dollars.
